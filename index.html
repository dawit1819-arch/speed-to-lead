QUESTIONS AGENTS ASK

Common questions

Is this real, or just a demo?

This page is a simulation, so nothing is sent. The real version uses the same steps with real tools: your ad's lead form, a phone system (Twilio or Kixie), your CRM and an AI that writes the notes. It can be set up for your team.

Do I have to change my CRM?

No. It connects to what you already use: Follow Up Boss, kvCORE, GoHighLevel and most others. New leads, notes and tasks land where you already work.

What does it cost to run?

Roughly $10–40 a month for the phone number and calls, plus a few cents per call for the AI notes. Your CRM and ads cost what they already cost. The setup is a one-time job.

What if I'm busy when a lead comes in?

The system calls you first. If you don't press 1 to accept, it can try the next agent on your team, or send the buyer a text saying you'll call back, and put a call-back task in your CRM.

Is it legal to record the calls in Massachusetts?

Massachusetts requires everyone on a call to agree to recording. The system says "this call is recorded" at the start of every call, and you can turn recording off and keep only your own notes.

Can it text people who didn't ask?

No. Texts only go to people who ticked the consent box on your form. Anyone who replies STOP is removed automatically.

Does the AI talk to my buyers?

No. You talk to your buyers. The AI only listens afterwards to write the summary, update the CRM and draft a follow-up email that you can check before it goes out.

What about leads at night or on weekends?

You choose. Leads outside your hours can get an instant text and a call first thing in the morning, so nobody waits until Monday.

How long does setup take?

About one to two weeks: connect the ad form, the phone number, the CRM and test it with a few fake leads before going live.

Who owns the data?

You do. Contacts, recordings and notes live in your CRM and your accounts, not ours.
